# 📊 Sales & Revenue Analysis Dashboard

### Excel Data Analysis Project

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes company revenue performance across multiple product categories over several years.

The goal is to identify trends, compare category performance, and generate insights that can support business decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze revenue trends over time
* Compare performance across product categories
* Identify top-performing products
* Evaluate yearly growth patterns
* Present insights in a clear, structured format

---

## 🗂️ Dataset Description

The dataset contains revenue data categorized by product type and year.

### Key Fields:

* Product Category (e.g., Accessories, Bikes)
* Year (2011–2016)
* Revenue
* Grand Total Revenue

---

## 📊 Data Structure

The dataset is organized as a pivot-style table showing:

* Revenue by category across years
* Total revenue per category
* Overall revenue trends

---

## 📈 Key Analysis

### 1. Revenue by Category

* Comparison of categories such as:

  * Bikes
  * Accessories

---

### 2. Yearly Revenue Trends

* Tracks how revenue changes from 2011 to 2016
* Identifies growth and decline periods

---

### 3. Top Performing Category

* Bikes appear to generate the highest revenue overall
* Accessories show steady growth over time

---

## 📊 Key Insights

* Revenue varies significantly across product categories
* Bikes consistently contribute the largest share of total revenue
* Some categories show strong growth trends over multiple years
* Revenue fluctuations highlight seasonal or market changes
